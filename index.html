01

Choose a team—or review

A supervised classifier maps messages to 77 support topics, then to six specialist teams. A review threshold avoids forcing a team choice on uncertain requests.

02

Show the specialist’s work

The selected team searches source-linked help guidance and prepares a next step. Visitors can inspect the activity trace and compare a simple tool-matching baseline.

03

Make actions safe to explore

For a lost-card request, visitors can confirm a simulated freeze. It changes only temporary demo state, with a visible result and audit entry.
